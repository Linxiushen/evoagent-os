# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in EchoWeave-RTC free from harassment for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the
community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, threats,
trolling, insults, publishing another person's private information, or using
project channels to facilitate impersonation, non-consensual cloning, fraud,
or abuse.

## Enforcement

Report project-level conduct concerns privately through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/Linxiushen/echoweave-rtc/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially;
a maintainer named in a report must not participate in its resolution.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Avoid public reports that
would expose a target's private information. Platform-level abuse can be
reported through [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
