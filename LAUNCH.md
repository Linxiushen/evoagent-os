# Launch Kit

**Pitch:** Durable workflows for AI agents that can crash, retry, wait for approval and prove what they produced.

**Release post:** EvoAgent Fleet v0.1 is a multi-agent control plane with DAG execution, capability matching, expiring leases, heartbeats, bounded retries, cost/token budgets, human approval, content-addressed artifacts, operational events and evidence-based route scoring.

**Demo:** run the release-readiness example; inspect research and review artifacts; show the publish node waiting for approval; terminate a worker after claim, expire its lease and let another worker complete it.

**Topics:** `multi-agent-systems`, `agent-orchestration`, `durable-execution`, `ai-agents`, `workflow-engine`, `human-in-the-loop`, `self-evolving-agents`
